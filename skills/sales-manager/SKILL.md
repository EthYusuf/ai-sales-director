## 📊 STEP 1: Winning Product Viability (The 7 Pillars)
1. **Problem-Solving Utility (Pain Relief):** Does it solve a real, urgent annoyance, save time, or prevent pain?
2. **"Wow" / Scroll-Stopping Factor:** Does it catch attention in the first 3 seconds of video feeds?
3. **Margin Health (3x Rule):** Can it sell for at least 3x the total product + landed shipping cost?
4. **Retail Scarcity:** Is it impossible to find on local convenience store shelves?
5. **Logistics Feasibility:** Under 500 grams? Non-fragile (no breakable glass/ceramic, liquids, or risky batteries)?
6. **UGC & Content Friendly:** Easy for creators to demonstrate "Before vs After"?
7. **Mass Market Appeal:** Scalable to hundreds of thousands of potential buyers?

**Rating Scale:**
- `90 - 100`: 🚀 **Mega Winner (Scale Immediately)**
- `75 - 89`: 📈 **Strong Potential (Profitable with the right hook)**
- `60 - 74`: ⚠️ **Moderate / Niche Risk (Requires bundling or custom offer)**
- `< 60`: ❌ **Avoid (Paid ads will struggle to break even)**

---

## 🌍 STEP 2: Target Country Viability (Where Will It Sell Most?)
- **Tier 1 Markets (Maximum Purchasing Power):**
  - **USA:** Impulse-buying powerhouse, highest market volume, fast shipping lines.
  - **UK:** High online penetration, compact logistics (5-8 days delivery), strong currency margin (£).
  - **Australia & Canada:** Lower ad CPMs than the US. Australia offers counter-seasonality (summer during northern winter).
  - **Germany & Netherlands:** Strong demand for functional quality. Local payments (Klarna, Sofort, iDeal) are crucial.
- **GCC / Gulf Markets (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar):**
  - Exceptional Average Order Value (AOV) for premium beauty, smart gadgets, and lifestyle items.
- **Markets to Avoid Initially:**
  - Regions with severe customs duties, delivery failure rates, or high cash-on-delivery return rates.

---

## 📱 STEP 3: Ad Channel Selection (Where Should Ads Run?)
- **TikTok Ads & Organic:** Visual gadgets, fast wow-factor demonstrations, Gen Z / Millennial audience.
- **Meta (Instagram Reels & Facebook Feed):** Problem-solving products, 25-55 age demographics, Advantage+ Shopping (ASC), and retargeting.
- **Google Shopping / Performance Max:** High search-intent products where buyers actively search for solutions.
- **Pinterest Ads:** Home decor, kitchen aesthetics, beauty, and female gift niches.

---

## 🎬 STEP 4: 3-Second Viral Ad Hook & Script
- **0:00 - 0:03 (Hook):** 3 distinct angles (Curiosity, Fear of Missing Out / Pain Point, Direct Comparison).
- **0:04 - 0:15 (Problem & Solution):** Visual agitation of the problem followed by seamless product intervention.
- **0:16 - 0:22 (Social Proof):** User reviews, ratings, and customer testimonials.
- **0:23 - 0:30 (CTA):** Limited-time urgency and discount call to action.

---

## 💰 STEP 5: Unit Economics & Break-Even ROAS
- **Selling Price:** Retail price
- **COGS (Product + Landed Shipping):** Supply cost
- **Payment Gateway Fees:** ~3.5% + $0.30
- **Gross Profit & Gross Margin %**
- **Break-Even ROAS:** `Retail Price / Gross Profit` (Every dollar spent above this ROAS is net profit).
- **Maximum Allowable CPA:** The maximum ad cost per order before losing money.
